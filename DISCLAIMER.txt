# Disclaimer

Many of the historical and theological texts addressed in this repository have remained inaccessible to the English-speaking world for more than a century. The volume of untranslated material, combined with the limited availability of specialized linguistic resources, makes conventional translation of the entire corpus impractical. AI-assisted methods provide a means of recovering this material at a scale that would otherwise be difficult to achieve.

This project is therefore best understood as a **salvage operation**: an effort to bring historically significant but effectively “locked” sources into contemporary discussion while preserving access to the original texts as the final point of reference.

**The original-language source texts remain authoritative.** The translations, annotations, interpretations, and analytical tools presented here are aids to study and should remain open to correction.

This repository contains experimental tools, workflows, translations, and example outputs for the analysis and interpretation of historical and theological texts using structured methods and AI-assisted processes.

The English translations were produced primarily with artificial-intelligence language tools operating under detailed translation instructions. The degree of subsequent review varies by text. Some translations received substantial comparison and correction, while others received only a cursory editorial review.

These translations should therefore be treated as **working translations rather than critical editions**. Errors, ambiguities, anachronisms, and interpretive distortions may remain. AI systems may mistranslate words, flatten historical distinctions, import modern assumptions, or produce interpretations not warranted by the source.

Accordingly, this project is intended for educational, research, and exploratory purposes. It is not a doctrinal authority and is not a substitute for trained theological, historical, or linguistic judgment, professional translation, pastoral discernment, or academic peer review.

The editor claims neither ecclesiastical authority nor interpretive finality. Readers using these materials in academic, ecclesial, pastoral, teaching, or publication contexts are strongly encouraged to consult the original sources and, where appropriate, qualified specialists.

**Every significant translation or interpretation should remain challengeable against the source.**

Use these materials with appropriate judgment and responsibility.
